The House / FAQ

QUESTIONS
WORTH ASKING.

Why is the collection called STAGES?

Because feelings move. A fragrance can capture a particular point in that movement, from curiosity to attraction, longing, memory and surrender.

Are the fragrances meant to be worn by gender?

No. STAGES is organised around emotion rather than gender. Wear the stage that speaks to you.

Why 30 ml?

A smaller format makes fragrance easier to carry, live with and finish. It also lets a collection remain playful rather than permanent.

Will there be more collections?

STAGES is Collection 01. The House is designed to grow through future chapters, each with its own visual language and story.
