THE HOUSE / FAQ

QUESTIONS
WORTH ASKING.

Why is the collection called STAGES?

Because feelings move. Each fragrance captures a point in that movement.

Are the fragrances gendered?

No. STAGES is organised around emotion rather than gender.

Why 30 ml?

A smaller format is easy to carry, easy to live with and keeps a collection playful.

What do the notes mean?

TOP describes the first impression, HEART the central character and BASE the drydown. Texture, temperature, projection and trail describe how the scent feels and behaves on skin.

Are these the actual ingredients?

No. Fragrance notes are an olfactive description and are not a substitute for the final INCI declaration. The actual ingredient list will be published separately once the final formula and compliance documentation are available.

How does the ₹50 first order offer work?

Every STAGES fragrance is ₹299 during the launch offer. On your first order, a flat ₹50 is deducted once at checkout.

What is the full collection price?

All nine STAGES fragrances are ₹2,499 together. That is ₹1,092 below the combined ₹3,591 MRP. First-time customers can bring the collection to ₹2,449.

How do I order?
