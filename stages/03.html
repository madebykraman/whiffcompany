AFTER THE NIGHT

KHUMAAR is the lingering hour. Keats gives intoxication a strange tenderness, a desire to disappear into sensation. This stage follows that same impulse: warmth, depth and the memory of a night refusing to end.

Wear it when the moment is over but you are not ready to leave it.
